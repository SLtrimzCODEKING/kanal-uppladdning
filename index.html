About our upload tool

We use a small private tool, running only on our own computer, to upload our own finished videos to our own YouTube channels, schedule them, set thumbnails, and read basic statistics (views and watch time) for our own videos. The tool uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API with our own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's channel or data.
